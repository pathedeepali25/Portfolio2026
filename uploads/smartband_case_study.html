A smartband and companion app built around the reality of how Indian women live, not how we imagine they should.

Type IoT + Mobile App
Focus Women's Health

The brief

One simple goal. Deceptively hard.

The ask seemed straightforward: design a smartband and app focused on women's health, and make it easy enough to use every single day.

But as we dug deeper, we realised the challenge wasn't technical. It wasn't even about features. It was about something far more stubborn, habit, priority, and the quiet way women are conditioned to put themselves last.

The problem

A truth no dashboard could show us

Before a single wireframe was drawn, we had to understand what we were actually solving for. And what we found was uncomfortable.

01
Women manage the household, the children, the work, and their own health comes last. Always.
02
Conditions like PCOS affect millions of Indian women, yet remain massively underdiagnosed and quietly ignored.
03
Health awareness alone doesn't work. Women know they should care. The behaviour still doesn't change.
"It wasn't about what they didn't know. It was about what they never had time, or permission, to do."

Research

We went where the data couldn't

The research phase took us out of the studio and into the field, into rural areas, real conversations, unfiltered ground reality. No surveys with neat dropdowns. Just listening.

What we heard
PCOS and nutritional deficiencies were common, but rarely talked about, let alone treated.
Meals were irregular, skipped, or eaten standing up after everyone else had finished.
Health was always the last item on the list, something to tend to once everything else was done.
The barrier wasn't information. It was behaviour, deep-set patterns that no health app had ever tried to understand.

This wasn't a features problem. This was a behaviour design problem, and that changed everything about how we built the product.

The solution

Four modules. One mission.

Instead of building another generic wellness app, we designed an ecosystem of four interconnected experiences, each one solving a specific, real friction point.

⚗
Onboarding that starts with truth
We don't guess what a user needs. We start with blood reports and actual diagnostics to identify real issues, then build a personalised reversal plan from the ground up. No generic advice, no assumptions.
↺
A reversal plan that fits real life
The core module delivers daily personalised tasks, not generic routines, but actions tailored to each user's specific condition. Simple, achievable, not overwhelming. Because a plan that's too hard is a plan that never starts.
▦
A dashboard for the rest of us
Health data is overwhelming, so we simplified it radically. Just "good" or "needs attention," with depth available for those who want it. Because clarity is the first step to action.
✉
A space that feels safe to talk
Talking about health, especially reproductive health, is deeply uncomfortable for many women. Our chatbot is designed to feel human, non-judgmental, and supportive. A space where there are no wrong questions.

A fifth layer, an education module drawing from Indian Vedic science, offered yoga, pranayam, and healthy recipes, with a UI deliberately inspired by Instagram and YouTube. Familiar interfaces, unfamiliar depth.

Design principles

The invisible work

Every design decision in this product passed through the same filter: does this make it easier for a woman to put herself first?

Not just tracking
The product goes beyond data collection to actually enabling change
Slow shift
It gradually teaches women that their health is worth prioritising
Radical simplicity
Complexity is the enemy of daily use, so we stripped it away
Behaviour first
What makes this valuable isn't the data, it's the action it inspires

"Health doesn't improve with data.
It improves with action."

This product tries to make that easier.
