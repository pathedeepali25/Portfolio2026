Experience3+ years, end-to-end

I'm Deepali, a product designer with 3+ years of experience, drawn to making things feel simple and clear. I like understanding what's really going on beneath a problem, not just what's visible.

Over time, I've learned that good design is more about removing than adding. I enjoy shaping experiences that feel intuitive, calm, and genuinely effortless to use.

03Capabilities

What I bring to a team.

Product thinking

  • Finding clarity, shaping unclear problems into something meaningful
  • Knowing what matters, focusing on decisions that actually move the needle
  • Understanding behavior, noticing where users hesitate, not just where they click
  • Simplifying deeply, removing friction instead of layering solutions
  • Thinking in trade-offs, balancing user needs with business reality

UX · UI execution

  • Designing flows, not just screens, including edge cases and states
  • Crafting interfaces, clean, structured, built for light and dark
  • Prototyping to think, using Figma (and code when needed) to explore ideas
  • Attention to detail, where spacing, type, and rhythm quietly matter
  • Making it usable, accessible, clear, and easy to navigate

Systems & collaboration

  • Building systems, components and patterns that scale with the product
  • Working with engineers, aligning early to make things real, not just ideal
  • Learning through research, asking, testing, and refining continuously
  • Communicating clearly, keeping teams aligned without overcomplicating
  • Taking ownership, from first question to final experience
